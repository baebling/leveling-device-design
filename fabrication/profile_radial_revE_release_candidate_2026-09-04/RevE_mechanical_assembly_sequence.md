# Rev E mechanical assembly sequence (release candidate)

> **SUPERSEDED — 조립에 사용 금지 (2026-10-02).** 이 문서는 과거 검토안이다. 아래의 1축 선시험 단계는 사용자의 일괄 주문·조립 조건과 맞지 않고, C04~C09 스토퍼는 허용되지 않은 추가 금속가공을 전제한다. `0~50 mm 밖 2 mm` 스토퍼 위치도 ±3° 전 자세와 실제 내부 리미트 접점을 검증하지 못했다. 현행 상태와 발주·제작 보류 사유는 [현재 범위 설명](README_CURRENT_SCOPE_2026-10-02.md) 및 [연속검증 기록](../../outputs/20261002_reve_followthrough/연속검증_중간기록.md)을 참조한다.

+Status: `NOT APPROVED FOR FABRICATION` until G-M01, G-M02, and G-C01 are closed.

+1. Cut and square the lower 4040 and upper 3030 profile sets. Match equal-length members before assembly.
+2. Assemble each rectangular frame loosely with the eight catalog corner brackets per frame, square diagonals, then torque progressively.
+3. Inspect C01-C03 hole coordinates against the frozen LMB-10 interface register before machining.
+4. Bolt each adapter to its specified lower cross-member slot. Install the LMB-10 with M8x16 fasteners only after the first-article pivot stack rotates freely.
+5. Assemble one actuator axis first. Check lower pivot free rotation, motor-body clearance, upper PHS6 articulation, tool access, and cable exit.
+6. Install the other two axes. Keep the upper frame supported independently until all three pins and retainers are installed.
+7. Install the three independent M10 mechanical stop assemblies and set them 2 mm outside the commanded 0-50 mm range.
+8. Move the unpowered mechanism through the 27-pose inspection matrix using controlled support. Record binding, contact, and fastener access.
+9. Apply witness marks after final torque. Do not use threadlocker until first-article adjustments are complete.
