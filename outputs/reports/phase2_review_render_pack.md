# Phase 2 review render pack

Date: 2026-08-27

Status: `RADIAL_3_CLEAN` preliminary review material. Not approved for fabrication.

## Purpose

This pack exposes the complete assembly and internal layout without changing the active CAD geometry. It is intended for concept review, interference discussion and assembly-sequence planning.

## Numbered assembly legend

1. Upper deck and HFS8 frame
2. Upper actuator joint packages
3. Three radial actuators A1-A3
4. Keyed central guide and compact Cardan
5. Lower actuator joint packages
6. Lower structural HFS8 frame
7. Universal lower interface plate
8. Parameterized cart receiver and mechanical latch interface

## Interpretation limits

- The exploded offsets are visual only and are not assembly clearances or installation dimensions.
- Orthographic renders are not dimensioned fabrication drawings.
- The cart receiver remains a parameterized interface dummy, not a cart-body design.
- LS-01 is a detailed CAD seed, but carrier/crosshead attachment, guarding, wiring, measured switch overrun and collar impact/push-off still require physical evidence.
