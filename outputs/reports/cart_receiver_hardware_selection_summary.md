# Cart receiver hardware selection summary

Status: Phase 1 preliminary. Not approved for fabrication.

CR-01-H4-S1 converts the prior connector-stack rules into a practical low-load hardware seed:

```text
master: 12 mm removable round locator + replaceable shoulder stop
secondary: Y-only locator in an X-slotted / diamond receiver
rest pad: 40 x 30 mm shim-adjustable Z seat
latch keeper: 5 mm steel retention keeper + separate mechanical secondary lock
```

At the existing 3x local screen load of about 558 N, the candidate bushing bearing is about 5.8 MPa, the replaceable stop bearing is about 1.7 MPa, stop bending is about 32.7 MPa, optional 6 mm dowel shear is about 19.7 MPa, and rest-pad contact is about 0.8 MPa. All pass their placeholder comparison screens.

The backing bridge remains conditional: add the 60 x 40 x 6 mm steel seed only after a 30-cycle mock-up shows shift greater than 0.2 mm, torque relaxation greater than 20%, marking, or latch-preload drift.

Exact purchased hardware, fits, torque, stop screw pattern, latch model, and sensor bracket location remain open. This is still `PHASE_1_PRELIMINARY_NOT_FOR_FABRICATION`.
