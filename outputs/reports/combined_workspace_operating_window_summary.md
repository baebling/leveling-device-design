# Combined Workspace Operating Window Summary

Status: Phase 1 preliminary. Not approved for fabrication.

The old 250 mm collapsed candidate remains geometrically possible for 0 to 100 mm lift and combined +/-3 degree pitch/roll, but only retains about 2.54 mm beyond a 15 mm actuator soft-end reserve at the lower extreme. It should not become the CAD baseline.

The recommended `WS-01-H20` candidate raises the collapsed device height to 270 mm, which is inside the user-confirmed 250 to 300 mm envelope. With a 195 mm outer and 220 mm inner keyed guide, the complete 0/50/75/100 mm and nine-corner +/-3 degree grid retains about 11.13 mm minimum actuator soft reserve and 88 mm minimum guide overlap. This is the pre-CAD parameter candidate, subject to physical actuator, yoke, bracket, and stop verification.
