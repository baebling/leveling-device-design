# Actuator bracket package summary

Status: Phase 2 detailed CAD seed updated 2026-08-27. Not approved for fabrication.

`JNT-BR-01-HRT8E` now uses the official HRT8E envelope: 8 mm bore, 23 mm body diameter, 11 mm eye width, M8x1.25 thread and 14 deg articulation. A conservative 14 deg projected-width screen needs about 16.24 mm, so the former 16 mm gap was superseded by an 18 mm detailed CAD seed. This leaves about 1.76 mm total numerical clearance.

At the revised 18 mm pin-support span, the regenerated screen gives about 8.7 MPa pin double shear and 78.5 MPa pin bending; 8 mm lugs give about 13.7 MPa bearing and 38.5 MPa root bending. The CAD includes replaceable 12x8.2x8 mm bushing envelopes, 3.5 mm misalignment spacers, retained M8 shoulder-pin envelopes and four M8 bracket attachment holes.

These values do not close V-01. Physical 14 deg two-plane and diagonal sweep, the real actuator-thread adapter, spacer/pin selection, bushing fit, attachment, fatigue and shock behavior remain open.
