# Travel Limit And Stop Summary

Status: Phase 1 preliminary. Not approved for fabrication.

The selected 270 mm workspace candidate is assigned a preliminary actuator-length hierarchy: 334 to 508 mm commanded soft range, electrical limit targets at 329 and 513 mm, and independent physical-stop guarded boundaries at 324 and 518 mm. These are position-planning values, not final switch or stop geometry.

The complete +/-3 degree / 0 to 100 mm grid remains inside the commanded range, with 11.13 mm minimum lower-side reserve. Two lift-stop contacts have a static reference of about 412 N each under the 42 kg active mass and 2.0 static factor, but impact capacity remains uncalculated. CAD must translate this hierarchy into external stop shoulders/collars and prove it with real actuator and limit-switch testing.
