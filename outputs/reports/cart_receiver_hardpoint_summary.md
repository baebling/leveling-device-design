# CR-01 Hardpoint Summary

Status: Phase 1 preliminary summary. Not approved for fabrication.

## Recommendation

Carry forward `CR-01-H1` as the hardpoint candidate for the CR-01 aluminum-profile receiver.

| Item | Candidate |
|---|---|
| Locator insert | 12 mm locator interface in 8 mm local steel insert or hardened bushing |
| Rest pad insert | 40 x 30 mm contact seed on 8 mm local pad with shim stack |
| Latch keeper | 5 mm local steel keeper seed |
| Profile fastening | two M8-class T-slot fasteners per critical hard point as a starting topology |
| Anti-slip | add dowel/key/positive stop after alignment |

## Screened Values

| Screen | Result |
|---|---:|
| Locator combined load | about 186 N |
| Locator insert bearing stress | about 1.94 MPa |
| Rest pad max load | about 331 N |
| Rest pad contact pressure | about 0.28 MPa |
| 4-latch keeper bearing stress | about 0.25 MPa |
| 2-latch keeper bearing stress | about 0.49 MPa |
| Insert-to-profile slip margin with placeholder clamp | about 6.45x |
| 6 mm local tab bending stress | about 16 MPa |

## Decision

- Bulk strength is not the main blocker in the active low-load PoC screen.
- The actual risks are slot slip, connector stiffness, repeated seating wear, alignment repeatability, latch clearance, and sensor/secondary-lock integration.
- Do not rely on slot friction as the final locator. Use a positive stop, dowel, or keyed plate after mock-up alignment.
